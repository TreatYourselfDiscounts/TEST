  • 🎲 The Random spinner looks up any place you type now, not just the ones in its list. A place that is not built in used to get claimed by a wrong idea if one of its words sat inside a place name somewhere else, so Luang Prabang showed Chiang Mai. Now any place that is not in the list gets its own live lookup card the moment you type it, with a Google Maps link, and the real matches still match
  • 🧳 The 3-dot menu on a trip card now has Duplicate trip and Merge with another trip.
