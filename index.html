About the Pond 🦆

Hello, I'm Lucy, the duck behind TreatYourselfDiscounts. This little pond is my corner of the internet — a place for deals, recipes, and cozy things that actually work.

Our Story

It started with a deal on a slow Sunday — and a duck who couldn't stop sharing. What began as a few coupon codes became a whole pond: deals, recipes, pantry lists, books, and a planner, all in one place. I still test every deal I share, and I only post what I'd buy myself (or gift to a friend).

What You'll Find Here

  • Deals — Real discounts, verified codes, and honest prices — no fake markdowns.
