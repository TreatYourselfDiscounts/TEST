One calendar file with this day's reminders, to-dos and meals. Apple Calendar and Outlook open it right up. Google Calendar takes it too: Settings, then Import & export, then Import. Each reminder above also has its own one-tap Google link.

Or go live with Google Calendar: connect your Google account and reminders you add here appear on your Google Calendar (deletes included), and your Google events show on each day page. A Google connection lasts about an hour at a time; tap Connect again when one runs out.
