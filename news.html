President Lee warns against blocking reform

President Lee Jae-myung said on Sunday that he understands people who lose out from reforms pushing back, but it is hard to tolerate public officials who speak of reform yet obstruct it. The message, his second in two days, signals a push for structural reform across government in his second year in office.

Source: Seoul Economic Daily

Top exam scorer admits cheating with AI

South Korea's top scorer in the national tax accountant exam admitted he used a hidden microcamera, a smartphone and generative AI to get answers. The government says it is preparing measures to prevent similar cheating at Thursday's national college entrance exam.
