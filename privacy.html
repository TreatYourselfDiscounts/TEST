Privacy Policy

Welcome to the Pond! This is the plain language version of what TreatYourselfDiscounts collects, what it does with it, and how to ask for it to be removed. Last updated November 13, 2026.

1. What we collect

When you create a Pond Account, we collect the email address you sign in with, the username you choose, and your avatar. You can also add an optional recovery email address, a spare address we only use if you lose access to the email on your account. When you use the site, we store the things you create and save, such as your recipes, deal lists, trips, pantry items, and settings. We also keep a simple count of page visits so we can see which pages people enjoy. We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or your payment details.

2. How we use it

Your account information is used to sign you in and to show you your own saved things on any device. Your recipes, lists, and other content stay private unless you choose to share them. Visit counts are used only to understand which pages are popular. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not show you ads based on your personal data.

3. Who helps us run the site

Your account and saved things are stored with Google Firebase, which provides our sign-in and database. Page visit counts are kept with GoatCounter, a privacy friendly counter that does not track you across the web. Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you shop through them, the shop may know you came from us, and TreatYourselfDiscounts may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, TreatYourselfDiscounts earns from qualifying purchases.

4. Cookies and local storage

This site stores small pieces of information in your browser, such as whether you are signed in, your theme and text size, and the layout choices you made. These stay on your device and are used only to make the site work the way you set it up. Clearing your browser data will reset them.

5. Children

This site is not made for children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not create an account or share personal information here.

6. Your choices and deleting your account

You can change or delete your saved things at any time from your account. If you would like your Pond Account removed entirely, email treatyourselfdiscounts@gmail.com from the email address on the account with the subject "Delete my Pond Account" and we will remove it. We will delete your account and everything tied to it: your username, avatar, bio, saved recipes, deal lists, trips, pantry items, planner entries, messages, comments, shares, friend requests, and notifications. We do not keep a copy of your personal data after deletion. Messages or shares that other members already copied or quoted may remain in their own copies. We aim to complete account deletions within 30 days of your request.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Pond grows. If it changes in an important way, we will say so on the home page. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

8. Contact

If you have a question about this policy or about your information, email treatyourselfdiscounts@gmail.com.
