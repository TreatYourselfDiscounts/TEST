Pond emails, in their own folder 📬

Deal alerts from the Pond live in the bell at the top of every page. Emails about your Pond account, like password resets, come from Google's sign-in service (the address ends in firebaseapp.com). Your email app is the one that sorts folders, so no website can file them for you at sign up. The good news: a one-minute filter in your email app files them forever, all by itself.

First, the trick that works everywhere

Every email app can make a rule that says: when mail comes FROM a certain address, put it in a folder. Open any email about your Pond account, note its From address (it ends in firebaseapp.com), and filter on that. The steps for the big email apps:

Gmail

1. Open an email about your Pond account.
2. Tap the three dots ⋮ and choose "Filter messages like these".
3. Tap "Create filter".
4. Check "Apply the label" and make a label called Pond. Check "Skip the Inbox" too if you want them out of your main inbox.
5. Tap "Create filter". Done: new Pond emails file themselves.

Apple Mail (iPhone and iPad)

iPhone Mail follows the rules you set in iCloud Mail:
1. Go to icloud.com/mail on any browser.
2. Tap the gear ⚙️, then Settings, then Rules, then "Add a rule".
3. Set: If a message is from an address that contains firebaseapp.com, then move it to a folder. Make a new folder called Pond.
4. Save. Your iPhone and iPad Mail apps will sort them from then on.

Outlook

1. Tap the gear ⚙️ for Settings, then Mail, then Rules.
2. "Add new rule": name it Pond.
3. Condition: From contains firebaseapp.com.
4. Action: Move to, and make a new folder called Pond. Save.

Yahoo Mail

1. Settings, then More Settings, then Filters, then "Add new filters".
2. Name it Pond. Set "From contains" to firebaseapp.com.
3. Choose "Move to folder" and make a folder called Pond. Save.

One more thing 💡

The same trick works for any sender you want corralled: store receipts, school emails, anything. And if you would rather skip email entirely, your Pond alerts are always waiting in the 🔔 bell at the top of every page and in My Account.
