About the Pond

Hello, I'm Lucy, the duck behind TreatYourselfDiscounts. This little pond is my corner of the internet — a place for deals, recipes, and cozy things that actually work.

Our story

It started with a deal on a slow Sunday — and a duck who couldn't stop sharing. What began with a few coupon codes became a whole pond: deals, recipes, pantry lists, books, and a planner, all in one place. I still test every deal I share, and I only post what I'd buy myself (or gift to a friend).

What you'll find here

  • Deals — Real discounts, verified codes, and honest prices — no fake markdowns. Prices are checked on November 15, 2026 and can change at the store.
  • Recipes — Lucy's cookbook: 262 recipes in the full cookbook, with 108 listed here with ingredients and steps.
  • Pantry and Planner — Keep track of what you have, what you need, and what's next.
  • Books and Travel — A nook for your shelves, and a planner for your trips.

Meet the Pond Crew
